Topics

  • Voice for contextSpeak the whole picture instead of typing a careful prompt, so the model can reason about what we are trying to do
  • LoopsGive the agent success criteria and a way to check its work, then let it repeat until it meets them
  • Adversaries and skepticsSubagents that attack the work, ideally a different model (the Codex plugin, a newer version, or another vendor)
  • Pictures and videoDiagrams and short videos that keep us caught up with what the agents have done

Voice for context

"One pattern I find useful for working with LLMs is a nice long ramble session. Sometimes the LLM needs more bits to understand what you're trying to achieve..."
Andrej Karpathy, on X, July 21, 2026
"Fun fact: I do most of my coding by speaking to Claude, rather than typing."
Boris Cherny, on X, March 29, 2026

Loops

"LLMs are exceptionally good at looping until they meet specific goals... Don't tell it what to do, give it success criteria and watch it go."
Andrej Karpathy, on X, January 26, 2026
"Probably the most important thing to get great results out of Claude Code: give Claude a way to verify its work. If Claude has that feedback loop, it will 2-3x the quality of the final result."
Boris Cherny, on X, January 2, 2026
